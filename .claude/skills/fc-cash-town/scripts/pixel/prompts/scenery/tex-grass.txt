A seamless tileable top-down pixel art texture for the ground of a cozy 2D town game: short lush green grass in a few greens, with darker tufts and a few tiny white and yellow flowers here and there. It fills the whole square image edge to edge, with no border, no frame, no objects, no characters and no shadows, seen straight from above. Chunky pixel art: every pixel is a clear square block about six screen pixels wide, a small palette of a few tones, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no text.
