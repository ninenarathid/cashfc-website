Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons for the egg and shearing games of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a cracked egg lying broken flat on the floor, its shell in two jagged halves and a yellow yolk in a small white puddle; 2) a pair of steel sheep shears, open, pointing up, with wooden handles wrapped in rope; 3) a soft white tuft of shorn wool, fluffy and round; 4) a small ragged white tuft of wool with a torn uneven edge; 5) a small heap of golden straw; 6) a small pink heart.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
