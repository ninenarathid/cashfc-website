A seamless tileable top-down pixel art texture for the ground of a cozy 2D town game: the bare stony ground of a mountain side, grey-brown earth with flat grey stones of different sizes set in it, small pebbles, a few thin cracks and a tuft of dry yellow-green grass here and there. It fills the whole square image edge to edge, with no border, no frame, no objects, no characters and no shadows, seen straight from above. Chunky pixel art: every pixel is a clear square block about six screen pixels wide, a small palette of a few tones, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no text.
