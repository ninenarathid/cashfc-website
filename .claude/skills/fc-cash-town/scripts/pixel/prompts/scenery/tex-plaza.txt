A seamless tileable top-down pixel art texture for the ground of a cozy 2D town game: a cobbled town plaza of rounded light warm grey stones of varied sizes, with darker grey gaps between them. It fills the whole square image edge to edge, with no border, no frame, no objects, no characters and no shadows, seen straight from above. Chunky pixel art: every pixel is a clear square block about six screen pixels wide, a small palette of a few tones, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no text.
