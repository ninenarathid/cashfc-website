A seamless tileable top-down pixel art texture for the ground of a cozy 2D town game: an untended farm field, dry warm brown earth with small clods and pebbles, overgrown here and there with little green weeds and a few pale dry grass tufts. It fills the whole square image edge to edge, with no border, no frame, no objects, no characters and no shadows, seen straight from above. Chunky pixel art: every pixel is a clear square block about six screen pixels wide, a small palette of a few tones, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no text.
