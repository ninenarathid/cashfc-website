Use the attached sprite sheet only as the reference for the pixel art style, its colours and its chunky square pixel size. Draw one scene that fills the whole canvas from edge to edge, with no border and no frame, for a mini-game of the same cozy 2D game.

A clearing in a mountain pine wood on a clear morning, seen flat and straight from the front by somebody standing in it. Along the bottom fifth of the picture the ground runs level from the left edge to the right edge: short green grass with brown fallen pine needles, a few small pine cones and two tiny wood chips. Behind the clearing, far back, a row of dark blue-green pine trees stands across the whole width, small and distant, their pointed tops reaching about half way up the picture, with a pale misty blue mountain ridge behind them. Above them the sky is one plain, flat, even pale blue with nothing in it. The whole middle of the picture, from the ground up to the top edge, is left open and empty: no tree, no trunk and no stump stands in the clearing itself (the game draws the tree there).

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, a thick dark outline on things, flat shading with a few tones per colour, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. No characters, no animals, no axe, no text, no letters, no numbers.
