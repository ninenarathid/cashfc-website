Use the attached pictures only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game: the first for the woodworkers' tools in the bag, the second for the insect house to draw again. Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
