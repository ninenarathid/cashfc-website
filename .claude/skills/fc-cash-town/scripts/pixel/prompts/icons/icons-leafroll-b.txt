Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons of leaf-rolling moths for the bag and the map of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Each insect fills its grid, is friendly and a little rounded, never frightening. Every moth is seen from above with its head at the top and its wings spread wide to the sides. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a small amber orange moth with two darker brown bands across its wings; 2) a small moth with rose pink wings edged in cream and a fluffy cream body; 3) a small moth with silver-grey wings marked with pale wavy lines; 4) a small moth with shiny copper brown wings and a dark spot on each forewing; 5) a small cream white moth with ink black spots scattered over its wings; 6) a small moth with deep brown wings and a round pale yellow spot on each hind wing like a little lantern.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
