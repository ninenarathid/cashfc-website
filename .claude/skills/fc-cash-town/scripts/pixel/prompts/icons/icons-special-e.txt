Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons for the farm's legendary finds kept in the bag of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. The eggs (icons 1, 2 and 5) have the same plump upright shell silhouette, width and height, seen from the side, as the eggs of the attached picture. Icons 1 to 4 are the rarest things of the farm, drawn at the same size as the others, in richer, deeper colours with more bright highlight pixels. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a crimson and gold egg crackled with bright orange-yellow lines like hot embers, a small steady three-tongued flame standing on its top like a crown, one small golden four-pointed sparkle star at its upper right corner; 2) a glossy black pearl egg with a deep-sea teal sheen and a faint pattern of small teal scales, one bold white shine at its upper left, one small golden four-pointed sparkle star at its upper right corner; 3) a whole golden fleece folded in two, a thick soft golden pelt covered in rows of bright shining curls, one small golden four-pointed sparkle star at its upper right corner; 4) a milky-white crystal standing upright with a deep navy core, a short trail of tiny white and pale blue sparkle squares streaming off one side like a comet's tail, one small golden four-pointed sparkle star at its upper right corner; 5) a red-orange egg crackled with dark lines like an ember, with tiny bright yellow spark pixels on it, one small golden four-pointed sparkle star at its upper right corner; 6) a shining golden tuft of fleece with a bold curl inside, one small golden four-pointed sparkle star at its upper right corner.

Every detached seed or sparkle is a connected cluster of at least two pixels on the icon's own grid, kept close to its own icon and within its column.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
