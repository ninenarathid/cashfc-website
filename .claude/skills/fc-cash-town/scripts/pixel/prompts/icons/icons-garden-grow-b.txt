Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons for the vegetable plots of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Each plant is seen from the front and a little above, and grows out of the same small low mound of dark brown soil at its foot, as the plants in the attached picture do. The young one of each pair is small and leafy and already has its own kind of leaf, with nothing on it to pick yet; the grown one beside it is the same plant, fuller, with its crop showing. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a young corn plant: one green stalk with long leaves, no ears yet; 2) a tall corn stalk with a pale tassel and two short ears of blue-purple kernels in green husks; 3) a small young tea bush of glossy dark green leaves; 4) a round tea bush of glossy dark green leaves with bright pale green new tips; 5) a young sunflower: a leafy stem with one closed green bud; 6) a tall sunflower with one big yellow flower with a dark brown centre.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
