Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons for the farm sheep's rare finds kept in the bag of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. The three wool locks (icons 1, 5 and 6) have the same soft round tuft silhouette, width and height as the tufts of the attached picture; only their colours and markings differ. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a soft tuft of wool in three solid bands of colour, dusty rose at its top, then rose-brown, then mocha brown at its bottom; 2) a small shed sheep's horn curled in a tight spiral, ivory with thin brown growth rings across it; 3) a big round extremely fluffy puff of pure white fleece made of many small round curls, with pale blue shading; 4) a golden dandelion seed clock: a round head of bright golden seed tufts on a short green stem, two tiny golden seeds drifting off it; 5) a white tuft of wool with a small rainbow arc of red, yellow, green and blue bands curving across it; 6) a navy-blue tuft of wool with one bright pale yellow shooting star and its short straight streak across it.

Every detached seed or sparkle is a connected cluster of at least two pixels on the icon's own grid, kept close to its own icon and within its column.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
