Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons for the vegetable plots of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Each plant is seen from the front and a little above, and grows out of the same small low mound of dark brown soil at its foot, as the plants in the attached picture do. They are modest garden helper plants grown beside the vegetables. The young one of each pair is small and leafy and already has its own kind of leaf, with nothing on it to pick yet; the grown one beside it is the same plant, fuller, with its crop showing. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a young marigold plant: a small bushy tuft of finely cut dark green leaves; 2) a bushy marigold plant with finely cut dark green leaves and three round flowers of layered orange-gold petals; 3) a young nasturtium plant: a few round, lily-pad-like pale green leaves on thin stalks; 4) a sprawling nasturtium plant with round lily-pad-like leaves and several bright orange-red trumpet flowers; 5) a young mint plant: a small tuft of bright green leaves with toothed edges; 6) a bushy mint plant: many upright stems thick with bright green toothed leaves, small pale lilac flower spikes at a few tips.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
