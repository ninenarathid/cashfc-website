Use the attached pictures only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game: the first for the goods of its shop, the second for the six things this sheet draws again, one at each place in its row. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
