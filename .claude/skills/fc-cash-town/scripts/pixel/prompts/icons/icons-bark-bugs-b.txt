Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons of insects that hide on tree bark and branches, for the bag and the map of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Each insect fills its grid, is friendly and a little rounded, never frightening. The four insects are seen from above with their heads at the top. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a small dark weevil with a round pale yellow spot on its back like a little lantern and a short snout; 2) a long dark red-brown longhorn beetle with very long banded feelers curving back over its body; 3) a round chafer beetle in shiny pink and pale green, a few cherry petals on its back; 4) a pale grey mantis patterned exactly like lichen-covered bark, its front legs folded, and one small golden four-pointed sparkle star at its upper right corner; 5) a short brown twig with two small dark beetles sitting on it; 6) a white cotton sheet folded into a neat square bundle tied with a brown string.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
