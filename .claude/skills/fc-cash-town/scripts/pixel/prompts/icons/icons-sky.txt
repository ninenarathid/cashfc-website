Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small user interface icons for that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Seven icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) dawn: a half sun rising over a green hill with a pink sky band; 2) morning: a bright sun with a small white cloud in front; 3) noon: a big bright yellow sun with rays; 4) afternoon: a warm orange sun with rays, a little lower; 5) dusk: an orange and red sun setting behind a hill line; 6) evening: a pale moon with a purple sky band and one star; 7) night: a yellow crescent moon with two small stars.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
