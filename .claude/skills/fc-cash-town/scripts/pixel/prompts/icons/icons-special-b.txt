Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons for the farm ducks' rare eggs and finds kept in the bag of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Every egg on this sheet (icons 1 to 5) has the same plump upright shell silhouette, width and height, seen from the side, as the eggs of the attached picture; only its shell colours, markings and small accents differ. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a white egg with three pale grey-blue wind streaks sweeping across it; 2) a dark green egg with a wet emerald sheen: a bright green shine stripe and one white shine pixel at its upper left; 3) a cream egg painted with two bold brush strokes, one orange and one teal; 4) a pale water-blue egg with an opaque shell, a tiny white rain cloud on its upper half and three small blue raindrops falling under it; 5) a glossy black egg with a silver-white crescent moon on its shell; 6) a small blank cream paper folded like a little paper boat, its edges soft and feathered, nothing written on it.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
