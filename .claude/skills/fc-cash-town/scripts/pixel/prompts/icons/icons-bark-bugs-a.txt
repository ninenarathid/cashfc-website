Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons of insects that hide on tree bark and branches, for the bag and the map of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Each insect fills its grid, is friendly and a little rounded, never frightening. The beetles, bugs and the moth are seen from above with their heads at the top; the treehopper and the stick insect are seen from the side facing left. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a small bark beetle checkered in black and white squares; 2) a slim pale green lacewing with large clear-looking wings made of solid opaque pale ivory pixels, veined in green, and big gold eyes; 3) a small brown treehopper whose back rises into a tall pointed thorn; 4) a dark brown assassin bug with a narrow head, a curved beak and orange bands on its legs; 5) a long thin stick insect covered in patches of green moss, its long legs stretched along its body; 6) a small moth with dew white wings and tiny solid pale blue dots with white highlights on them like drops of water, and one small golden four-pointed sparkle star at its upper right corner.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
