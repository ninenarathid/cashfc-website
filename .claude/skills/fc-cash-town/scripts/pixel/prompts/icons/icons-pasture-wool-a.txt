Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons for the wool shorn from the farm's sheep of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Every fleece across both wool sheets has the same soft round tuft silhouette, width and height; only its colour and internal curl pattern differ. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a soft white tuft; 2) a tuft in patches of brown, fawn and grey; 3) a tuft patched black and white; 4) a white tuft filled with bold fluffy curls; 5) a cream fleece tuft with pale yellow centres in its fluffy lobes like dandelion seed heads; 6) a tuft in seven bands of colour from red to violet.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
