Use the attached pictures only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game: the first for the plants growing out of their small mounds of soil, the second for the paper seed packet. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons for the vegetable plots and the bag of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. The first four are plants, each seen from the front and a little above, growing out of the same small low mound of dark brown soil at its foot, as the plants in the first picture do; the young one of each pair is small and already has its own kind of leaf, with nothing on it to pick yet, and the grown one beside it is the same plant, fuller, with its crop showing. The last two are for the bag, with no soil. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a young fig tree: a short smooth pale grey trunk with three big dark green leaves, each leaf with three deep rounded lobes like an open hand; 2) a small fig tree: a short smooth pale grey trunk forking into two branches under a rounded crown of big dark green hand-shaped lobed leaves, with two clearly visible small pear-shaped brownish-purple figs sitting close against the branches among the leaves, each smaller than a whole leaf, not hanging on stalks; 3) young garlic chives: a small clump of short flat green leaves; 4) a clump of long flat green garlic chive leaves with three thin stalks rising above them, each topped by a small round ball of tiny white star-shaped flowers like a little white pompom, with no yellow in them; 5) one whole pear-shaped brownish-purple fig with a short stalk, and beside it half a fig cut open showing pink-red flesh full of tiny cream seeds; 6) a small paper seed packet with a sky blue band across its top and half a fig cut open, pink-red inside, drawn on its front.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
