Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons of small things that fall from a shaken tree branch, for a mini-game of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Each thing is small and simple. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a short broken brown twig with one small fork; 2) one green leaf tumbling, tilted; 3) three small pink cherry petals close together; 4) one dry curled brown leaf; 5) a short sprig of dark green cedar needles; 6) a small tuft of loose white silk threads.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
