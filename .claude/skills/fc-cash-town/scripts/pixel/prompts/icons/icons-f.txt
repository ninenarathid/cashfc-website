Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small user interface icons for that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Four icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) two joined eighth music notes in warm gold; 2) the same two music notes crossed out by a thick red diagonal bar; 3) a small loudspeaker with one short sound wave (quiet); 4) a small loudspeaker with three sound waves (loud).

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
