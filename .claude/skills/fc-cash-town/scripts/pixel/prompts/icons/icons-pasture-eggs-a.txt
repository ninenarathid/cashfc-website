Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons for the farm animals' eggs kept in the bag of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Every egg across all three egg sheets has the same plump upright shell silhouette, width and height, seen from the side; only its shell colours, markings and small identifying accents differ. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a pale brown egg with a wisp of golden straw stuck to its shell; 2) a soft cream egg; 3) an egg with a pale sky-blue shell; 4) an egg with a dark chocolate-brown shell speckled with darker freckles; 5) an amber egg with a round pale yellow patch in its middle like a warm light inside; 6) a milk-white egg resting on two small white cloud puffs.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
