Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons for the vegetable plots of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Icons 1, 2, 4 and 5 are plants, each seen from the front and a little above, growing out of the same small low mound of dark brown soil at its foot, as the plants in the attached picture do. The young one of each pair is small and leafy and already has its own kind of leaf, with nothing open on it yet; the grown one beside it is the same plant, fuller, with its crop showing. Icons 3 and 6 are the flowers those plants give, picked, each on its own with no soil and no mound, drawn as large as the other icons. The golden dandelion is a rare flower: its gold is warm and shining, with a few bright highlight pixels. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a young golden dandelion plant: a flat rosette of long, deeply toothed green leaves with two small closed golden-green buds on short stems; 2) a grown golden dandelion plant: the same toothed rosette, one open flower of many thin shining golden petals and two round, airy golden seed clocks on tall hollow stems; 3) one picked golden dandelion on its own: a round open flower of many thin shining golden petals on a short hollow green stem with one small toothed leaf; 4) a young marigold plant: a small bushy tuft of finely cut dark green leaves; 5) a bushy marigold plant with finely cut dark green leaves and three round flowers of layered orange-gold petals; 6) one picked marigold flower on its own: round, of layered orange-gold petals, on a short green stem.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
