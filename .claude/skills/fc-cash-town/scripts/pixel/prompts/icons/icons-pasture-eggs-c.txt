Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons for the farm's eggs and dairy kept in the bag of that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Every egg across all three egg sheets has the same plump upright shell silhouette, width and height, seen from the side; only its shell colours, markings and small identifying accents differ. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping:
1) a pale blue egg with an opaque shell, a wavy darker blue water line across its middle; 2) a pearl-white egg with a pale yellow crescent moon on its shell; 3) a white egg with an angular paper-pleat pattern on its shell in grey lines; 4) a deep sea-blue egg patterned with small teal scales, one small golden four-pointed sparkle star at its upper right corner; 5) thick white cream heaped in a small brown earthenware cup; 6) a pale yellow block of butter half wrapped in white cloth tied with string.

Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
