Use the attached picture only as the reference for the pixel art style, the colours and the thick dark outline of a cozy 2D town game. Draw a new sprite sheet of small icons for the lights that stand in the middle of a floating flower raft for a river festival in that game, in the same pixel art style.

Each icon is drawn on its own 16 by 16 pixel grid, scaled up big so every pixel of the icon is a large crisp square, all icons the same size, simple and bold so they read clearly when shown small, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Six icons in one row across the canvas, evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, not touching, not overlapping. Icons 1 to 3 are one candle and icons 4 to 6 are one jar, each drawn three times exactly alike in size, shape and colour, so that they are plainly one thing, light from the upper left. Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no frames or boxes around the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers.
