Every piece is seen exactly from the front, flat, with no perspective and no tilt. Every piece has an outline perfectly symmetrical left to right and top to bottom, while its top and bottom shading may differ as specified below, its edges straight and of even thickness, and its middle one flat colour with nothing drawn in it. All pieces share the same pixel size and grid alignment across the sheet. Square pieces are 24 by 24 pixels and wide low buttons are 24 by 12 pixels, scaled up big so every pixel is a large crisp square, with a thick dark outline and two or three tones per colour. Each piece has a three-pixel-wide rim, including its outline, and three by three pixel corner blocks. Each edge between the corner blocks consists only of straight, constant colour bands along its entire length, with no grain, mottling or decoration. Crisp pixel art with hard square pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no glow. Transparent background, no shadows under the pieces, no icons, no text, no letters, no numbers.
