# The site as a game

The site already has many of a game's parts. This page names them, finds
the missing ones, and says how to add each without breaking what works.
Check `git log` before relying on a line here: the site ships almost daily.

## What the site already is

| In a game | On the site |
|---|---|
| The world | The FC's restaurant: the board, the gallery, the party finder |
| A player | A member with a verified character |
| A player card | The member page: portrait, colour, bio, badges, showcase, raids, collections |
| Guild | The FC; ranks from Dishwasher to Chef Toumant; "On vacation" |
| Friendly currency | Popoto: one per friend per day, thrown with an animation |
| Loot with rarity | Rare popoto (R, SR, UR), each with a member-written line |
| Achievements | Admin-made badges as foil plaques; the playstyle grades (Legendary, Master, Expert) |
| Leaderboards | Two popoto boards and eight playstyle boards |
| Raids and dungeons | The party finder: seats, roles, loot plans, outcomes and group photos |
| Live events | Festival glamour contests, Evercold, the domain vote |
| Rewards | Prize draws, the gil wallet, handed over in person by Aqua |
| Daily ritual | The 07:00 popoto reset, the bell, birthdays |
| News | The activity feed (from the game), the timeline, announcements |
| NPCs | Aqua (on the prize cards), and the popoto as a mascot |

## What a game would have that the site lacks

1. **A first-day path (onboarding as a quest).** "First day at the
   restaurant":
   - verify your character;
   - pick a colour;
   - send your first popoto;
   - join or create a party;
   - post a GPose.

   Each step is ticked by real data, with a small badge at the end. This
   uses endowed progress (verifying already counts) and leads to the core
   loops.
2. **A reason to explore.** Hidden things: Easter eggs, secret flavours,
   discovering features. Explorers currently have little to find.
3. **Site-native progression.** A title or level for *site* play (helping,
   hosting, decorating, photographing), not for raids, which the game
   already ranks. It should be horizontal: new cosmetics and roles, not
   power.
4. **Quests and orders.** In the restaurant theme, "orders of the day":
   - small, optional, cooperative prompts (throw a popoto to someone you
     haven't before; cheer a clear);
   - rotating, capped, and never punishing;
   - written once per season, so that it runs itself.
5. **Seasons.** A seasonal arc (Halloween, Starlight, Songkran) with a
   cosmetic track for everyone, earned by playing, with no pass to buy.
   Each ends with a peak-end recap.
6. **A shared world to be in together.** Today members visit pages, not
   each other. That is the gap **Cash Town** fills (the fc-cash-town skill).
7. **A spendable earned currency,** separate from popoto (see economy.md),
   only when there is something worth spending it on. Cash Town's mall,
   decorations and seeds are the natural first sinks.
8. **Returning-player flow.** A welcome back, a "what you missed" recap, no
   penalty, and an easy first action. Two thirds of the roster is "On
   vacation".

## Principles for gamifying this site

- **Gamify the friendship, not the clicks.** Reward moments between people
  (thanks, help, playing together), never page views or time on site.
- **Cosmetic and horizontal first.** Expression is cheap, fair and endless.
  Power progression is none of those.
- **The game is the FC, and the site is its clubhouse.** Mechanics should
  send people back into FFXIV together (parties, events, photos), not keep
  them on the website.
- **Small daily, bigger seasonally, and nothing mandatory.** Two minutes a
  day is a full experience. An hour on a festival night is a great one.
  Zero for a month is fine.
- **Every system needs an admin story.** Who runs it, in how many minutes a
  week, with which panel?

## Map of motivations, to check coverage

| Motivation | Today | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Community | popoto, comments, parties, tags | being *together* in one place (Cash Town) |
| Competition | playstyle boards, the contest podium | friendly, opt-in, low-stakes mini-games |
| Challenge and strategy | raids (in-game), the guide quiz | short skill games |
| Completion | rare popoto, collections | site-native sets (flavours, badges, seasonal cosmetics) |
| Fantasy and story | the restaurant theme | the town, NPCs, seasonal storylines |
| Design and creativity | glamours, profile colour, showcase | homes and decoration, farm layouts |
| Discovery | rare lines | hidden things, secret areas |
