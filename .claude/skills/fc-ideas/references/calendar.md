# The FC's year: FFXIV, Thailand and the FC itself

Typical windows only. **Look up this year's exact dates** (the Lodestone
news, or a web search) before planning around one: seasonal events move by
a week or two every year, and patch dates are announced in the Letters from
the Producer LIVE.

## FFXIV seasonal events (typical)

| Month | Event | Hooks for the site |
|---|---|---|
| late Dec – mid Jan | **Heavensturn** (the new year; a zodiac animal) | a new-year popoto, FC Wrapped, resolutions |
| Feb | **Valentione's Day** | popoto with notes, pair glamours, a "thank you" week |
| early Mar | **Little Ladies' Day** | a cheer or idol glamour theme |
| Mar – Apr | **Hatching-tide** (eggs) | a hidden-egg (hidden popoto) hunt |
| summer (varies) | **Make It Rain** (Gold Saucer) | the Gold Saucer playstyle board, a live raffle |
| Aug | **Moonfire Faire** | a beach and summer glamour contest |
| late Aug – Sep | **The Rising** (FFXIV's anniversary, 27 Aug) | "how long have you played" cards |
| mid Oct – early Nov | **All Saints' Wake** (Halloween). It ran Oct 18 – Nov 4 in 2024 and Oct 17 – Nov 4 in 2025 (consolegameswiki). | a spooky skin, the Halloween glamour contest. The contest engine is ready, but contest 1 is an admin-only **sample** full of fake looks: create the real one fresh. |
| mid – late Dec | **Starlight Celebration** | Secret Popoto, snow skin, year-end memories |
| several times a year | **Moogle Treasure Trove** | a "grind together" goal and party board |
| before each patch | **Letters from the Producer LIVE** | a watch party, countdowns |

**The patch rhythm.** Each expansion's x.0 is followed by major patches about
every 3–4 months, with smaller x.x5 parts between. A new savage tier or
ultimate brings a prog season: the party finder and the feed get busy, and a
"first clear" celebration fits. The site currently tracks the tier as
M9S–M12S. The pipeline switches to a new tier automatically.

## Thailand

| When | Occasion | Hooks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Jan | New Year | Wrapped, a fresh-start card |
| Jan / Feb (lunar) | Chinese New Year | red and gold skin, a "lucky" popoto |
| 14 Feb | Valentine's Day | alongside Valentione's |
| 13–15 Apr | **Songkran** | the throw splashes water, a Songkran glamour contest |
| 12 Aug | Mother's Day | thank-you notes |
| Oct | Halloween (popular) | alongside All Saints' Wake |
| Nov (full moon of the 12th lunar month; around 24–25 Nov in 2026, to be verified) | **Loy Krathong** | a krathong popoto, making wishes |
| 5 Dec | Father's Day | thank-you notes |
| late Dec | New Year countdown | alongside Starlight and Heavensturn |

## The FC and the site

- **The site went live on 2026-08-24** (v2 in this repo). Its first
  birthday falls around The Rising.
- **The FC's own founding date:** ask the user. It is the best anniversary
  for FC Wrapped.
- **Evercold** (the popoto draw) runs 2026-09-09 → 2026-10-09.
- **The domain run-off** ends 2026-10-02 00:57 Bangkok time; the move to the
  new domain follows.
- **Festival contests** are run by Aqua, one per festival. Her rulings are in
  the glamour-contest memory note.
